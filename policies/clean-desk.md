# Cash Capital Bank — Information Security Policy
## Document Ref: CCB-POL-SEC-003: Clean Desk and Clean Screen Policy

| v1.0 | June 2026 | Joseph Agyapong | Chief Information Security Officer | Public / Internal |

### 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to reduce the risk of a physical data breach or unauthorized visual compromise of sensitive customer and financial information. This policy satisfies the ISO 27002 control objective: *To prevent loss, damage, theft, or compromise of sensitive data*.

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to all working environments within Cash Capital Bank facilities, including retail branch desks, corporate offices, call centers, and home-office environments utilized for remote work.

### 3. Policy Statements

#### 3.1 Clean Screen Requirements
* Users must lock their workstation screens manually whenever they step away from their desks (Windows Key + L).
* **Automated Enforcements:** All corporate laptops and thin clients will be hardcoded via Group Policy Object (GPO) to trigger a password-protected screensaver after **5 minutes of inactivity**.
* Screen filters (privacy screens) must be utilized by personnel operating in public-facing retail bank branches or open office environments handling Non-Public Personal Information (NPI).

#### 3.2 Clean Desk Requirements
* All high-classification physical documentation, customer application forms, credit reports, and account files must be cleared from desks and placed in locked filing cabinets when unattended.
* Post-it notes, notebooks, or desktop accessories containing corporate passwords, PINs, or sensitive operational credentials are strictly prohibited.
* Mass storage media, including encrypted USB drives or external hard disks, must not be left exposed on desks and must be stored in a secure lockbox at the end of each business day.

#### 3.3 Disposal of Media
* Physical printouts containing sensitive customer data or banking transactions must never be thrown into standard trash bins. 
* Employees must utilize designated secure shredding bins or commercial cross-cut shredders located in each department for disposal of non-record sensitive paper media.

### 4. Enforcement and Compliance
The Information Security team will conduct unannounced, periodic physical walk-throughs of corporate offices after hours. Violations will result in formal warning tickets logged against the employee's operational file.
